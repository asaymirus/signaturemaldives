## 📧 Gmail Integration Guide

### Quick Setup Steps

1. **Copy Your Signature**
   - Click the green "Copy to Gmail" button in the preview
   - Your signature HTML is now copied to clipboard

2. **Open Gmail Settings**
   - Visit Gmail.com
   - Click the Gear Icon → Settings
   - Go to the Forwarding and POP/IMAP tab

3. **Paste Your Signature**
   - Scroll to the Signature section
   - Click in the text area
   - Paste your signature (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V)

4. **Save Changes**
   - Click "Save Changes" at the bottom
   - Your signature is now active!

### Pro Tips

- You can create multiple signatures and switch between them
- The signature appears on new emails (not replies by default)
- You can edit your signature anytime
- Gmail supports HTML signatures, so your formatted signature will appear exactly as designed
- Remember to save after making changes

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Signature not showing in Gmail

1. Make sure you clicked "Save Changes" in Gmail settings
2. Refresh your Gmail page
3. The signature appears on NEW emails, not replies
4. Check if you selected the signature for your account

### Formatting looks different

1. Some email clients don't support all HTML features
2. Test your signature by sending yourself a test email
3. Adjust colors and fonts if needed

### Can't copy signature

1. Make sure JavaScript is enabled
2. Try using a different browser
3. Try copying as "Copy as HTML" and pasting manually
